05.Sunny Path

"Sunny Path" is a narrative visual essay inspired by a segment of my favorite poem by Korean writer Byung Ryul Lee. The primary goal of this project was to convey the emotions I experienced while engaging with the poem through visual storytelling. Walking on the sunny path or walking on the shady path, Which one do I prefer? That’s how I understood love. The sunny path or the shady one.
